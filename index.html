    How to search

    • Add your niche: include "NHS", "healthcare" or "public sector" in searches.
    • Not London only: about half the UK's AI talent is in London, so competition there is fiercest. Also search Manchester, Leeds, Birmingham, Bristol and "remote".
    • Use NHS Jobs: jobs.nhs.uk. Set alerts for digital, automation, RPA and informatics roles.
    • Skip the junior queue: lead with shipped, tested, deployed projects and your inside-route pilot, not a "junior developer" label.
